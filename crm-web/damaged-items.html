Damaged Items

📉 What is this page?

Use this page when stock leaves your shop without being sold — because it broke, expired, went missing, or was given away.

Example: you bought 24 bottles and 3 fell and broke. You did not sell those 3, but they are gone. Record them here and your stock drops from 24 to 21 — with a reason saved, so you know later why the number changed.
💡 Not for sales or returns. If a customer bought it, record a sale. If a customer brought it back, use Sales Return. Use this page only for stock you lost.
