Person
Unsubscribing sets the person’s unsubscribed flag in Customer.io; deleting removes their profile and its history there. Either way the CRM lead stays where it is.
After an unsubscribe the two systems disagree, and they are meant to: Customer.io says the person is unsubscribed, the lead in the CRM still says they are not. Anything built on the CRM copy — a segment, a list, an export — will not know until someone changes the lead as well. An address Customer.io has never seen is refused here rather than created.
