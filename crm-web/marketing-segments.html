Segments in Customer.io

A segment is a set of rules over what the shop already knows about a customer — orders, money, country, the products of the last order, whether they are on the insider list. The same numbers travel to Customer.io with every profile update, so a segment here and a filter there mean the same thing. Sync creates a manual segment in Customer.io the first time and keeps its membership in step afterwards. Test and service addresses — the smoke orders of our own releases, QA carts, the shop's own mailbox — are left out of every count here and are never sent to Customer.io.

New segment

A rule can only be true about a value we have: someone with no country recorded is not "not from Italy", and such a person is left out of the segment. Use “is known” to talk about people we have no value for. A segment with no rules matches nobody.
