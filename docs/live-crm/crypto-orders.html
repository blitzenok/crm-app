Crypto payments — verified on-chain automatically, ship blocked until verified

The sidecar checks the blockchain every ~90 s (read-only). An order is released only when a matching USDT transfer to our wallet has enough confirmations and the sender passed sanctions screening. Each order has a unique amount (cents offset) so payments can be matched. Unpaid orders are cancelled 60 minutes after checkout (+60 min if the customer pressed "I've sent the payment"). Refunds are manual: send from the wallet yourself, then record the refund tx here. The system never sends crypto.
